Standard Reference Ranges for Common Lab Tests

Note: Reference ranges may vary slightly between laboratories. The ranges listed here are typical adult values. Pediatric, pregnancy, and age-specific ranges may differ.

COMPLETE BLOOD COUNT (CBC)
WBC (White Blood Cell Count): 4.5-11.0 x10^3/uL
RBC (Red Blood Cell Count) Male: 4.7-6.1 x10^6/uL
RBC (Red Blood Cell Count) Female: 4.2-5.4 x10^6/uL
Hemoglobin Male: 13.5-17.5 g/dL
Hemoglobin Female: 12.0-16.0 g/dL
Hematocrit Male: 38.3-48.6%
Hematocrit Female: 35.5-44.9%
Platelet Count: 150-400 x10^3/uL
MCV: 80-100 fL
MCH: 27-33 pg
MCHC: 32-36 g/dL
RDW: 11.5-14.5%

BASIC METABOLIC PANEL (BMP)
Glucose (fasting): 70-100 mg/dL
BUN: 7-20 mg/dL
Creatinine Male: 0.7-1.3 mg/dL
Creatinine Female: 0.6-1.1 mg/dL
Sodium: 136-145 mEq/L
Potassium: 3.5-5.0 mEq/L
Chloride: 98-106 mEq/L
CO2: 23-29 mEq/L

COMPREHENSIVE METABOLIC PANEL (CMP) - additional tests
Calcium: 8.5-10.5 mg/dL
Total Protein: 6.0-8.3 g/dL
Albumin: 3.5-5.5 g/dL
Total Bilirubin: 0.1-1.2 mg/dL
Alkaline Phosphatase: 44-147 U/L
AST: 10-40 U/L
ALT: 7-56 U/L
eGFR: >60 mL/min/1.73m2

LIPID PANEL
Total Cholesterol: <200 mg/dL desirable
LDL Cholesterol: <100 mg/dL optimal
HDL Cholesterol: >40 mg/dL (men), >50 mg/dL (women)
Triglycerides: <150 mg/dL normal
VLDL: 2-30 mg/dL

THYROID PANEL
TSH: 0.4-4.0 mIU/L
Free T4: 0.8-1.8 ng/dL
Free T3: 2.3-4.2 pg/mL
Total T4: 4.5-12.0 ug/dL
Total T3: 80-200 ng/dL

DIABETES
Fasting Glucose: 70-100 mg/dL (normal), 100-125 (prediabetes), 126+ (diabetes)
HbA1c: <5.7% (normal), 5.7-6.4% (prediabetes), 6.5%+ (diabetes)

IRON STUDIES
Iron: 60-170 ug/dL
Ferritin Male: 20-500 ng/mL
Ferritin Female: 20-200 ng/mL
TIBC: 250-370 ug/dL

VITAMINS AND MINERALS
Vitamin D: 30-100 ng/mL
Vitamin B12: 200-900 pg/mL
Folate: 2.7-17.0 ng/mL
Magnesium: 1.7-2.2 mg/dL
Phosphorus: 2.5-4.5 mg/dL

OTHER
Uric Acid Male: 3.0-7.0 mg/dL
Uric Acid Female: 2.5-6.0 mg/dL

Understanding Lab Flags:
H or High: Value is above the reference range
L or Low: Value is below the reference range
HH or Critical High: Value is dangerously above normal
LL or Critical Low: Value is dangerously below normal
A or Abnormal: Value is outside the reference range

Factors Affecting Results:
- Fasting status: Glucose and triglycerides require 8-12 hour fasting for accurate results
- Medications: Many drugs affect lab values
- Time of day: Some tests (cortisol, testosterone) vary throughout the day
- Hydration: Dehydration can falsely elevate many values
- Exercise: Intense exercise can temporarily affect muscle enzymes, glucose, and other values
- Diet: Recent meals affect glucose, triglycerides, potassium, and phosphorus
